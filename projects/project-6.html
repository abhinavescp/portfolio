Portfolio Diversification via Geopolitical Arbitrage

Sayano Deutschland GmbH, Business Viability and Pricing Strategy

This is a personal portfolio and not an official publication of Sayano Deutschland GmbH. Views are my own.

Role Contributor
Employer Sayano Deutschland GmbH
Tools Porter's Five Forces, Ansoff's Growth Matrix
Headline result Contributed to 33 percent annual revenue growth

The strategic challenge

The Russia Ukraine conflict pushed energy costs sharply higher in some regions and lower in others. A ceramics business needed to decide fast whether to hold its position or shift into a new product line.

What the project delivers

Using Porter's Five Forces and Ansoff's Growth Matrix, the analysis identified a window to pivot part of the ceramics portfolio into glassware, taking advantage of a German energy cost arbitrage created by the conflict.

This turned a period of market uncertainty into a source of new margin, by moving quickly on the opportunity.

Results

MIAMIO is a brand of Sayano Deutschland. I contributed to 33 percent annual revenue growth by applying quantitative methods to identify a new, data driven market segment opportunity.

Sourcing the same product from China gave a gross margin of 71 percent, against 28 percent had it been produced in the EU, a difference of 43 percentage points.
